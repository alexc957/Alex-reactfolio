# Getting Started with AI Agents: Claude & Copilot

AI is transforming the way we build software. The **agentic paradigm** — where AI agents autonomously plan, execute, and iterate on tasks — is reshaping the developer workflow.

## What are AI agents?

Unlike traditional code completion tools, AI agents like **Claude** and **GitHub Copilot** can:

- Understand complex, multi-step requirements
- Plan a solution before writing code
- Execute across multiple files
- Adapt when things go wrong

## My experience

Working with Claude and Copilot in an agentic workflow has been a game-changer for productivity. Instead of writing boilerplate, I focus on:

1. **Architecture decisions** — guiding the agent toward the right design
2. **Code review** — ensuring quality and correctness
3. **Integration** — connecting AI-generated pieces with existing systems

## Key takeaway

> The future isn't AI replacing developers — it's developers who leverage AI agents outpacing those who don't.

Stay tuned for a deeper dive into specific workflows and patterns.
